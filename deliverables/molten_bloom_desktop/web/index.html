Welcome to Molten Bloom Studio

Live visuals that listen to your music. Three steps get you going:

1Connect the soundPick Track (an audio file) or Line in (your mixer) in the bottom-right panel. Clock works without any audio.
2Pick a lookClick a mood on the left, or press Auto and let the Studio run the show by itself.
3Send it outOpen output puts a clean window on the projector. Spout sends it to Resolume, TouchDesigner or OBS.

New here

Automation (bottom deck): draw how any setting changes over a whole track. Synesthesia (Color): the music's notes pick the colours. Record whole track (Track source or Export): records start to end with the audio, then stops by itself.
